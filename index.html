01 · About

I build technology, and I teach it.

The work

I am a software engineer and technology builder from Cameroon. I take an idea from a rough concept through design, systems, and a product someone can use. The work now sits in software, AI, mobile applications, computer vision, and the places where a person meets a machine.

I founded JACKBASE TECH and lead the engineering there. That is where I develop products and try ideas that are not finished yet. I also work as Tech Engineer at D-CAT Center, supporting the technical side of the hub, and I built the center’s website.

Where it started

The first work was close to the machines. Hardware needed repair, laboratories had to stay running, and I was often the person explaining the lesson. I maintained computers, taught computer science, and trained people in technical skills, including hands-on IoT and AI.

I taught and supported machines at Progressive Comprehensive High School, at LORDa, and at Firm Foundation College of Art Science and Technology. From February 2021 to May 2023 I taught hands-on IoT and AI at Central University Institute in Bamenda. From 2021 to 2024 I also gave international online training in artificial intelligence, and I have mentored young people on STEM projects. Serving as an educational prefect was an early lesson in being responsible for other people.

I studied computer software engineering at the National Polytechnic University Institute in Bamenda, first through an HND and then a bachelor’s degree. That study was the foundation. Hardware, circuit boards for AI projects, IoT, and robotics remain one layer of the background.

Products

Software engineering became the center of the practice. Smaller applications grew into products with accounts, data, messaging, and a path onto a server. At JACKBASE TECH that includes SaaS, web and mobile applications, automation, and computer vision. The interface is one part of the job. The rest is the API, the data, the deployment, and the problem the product has to solve.

Cipher SMS shows the shift. It began as a school tool and became an online platform for students, teachers, attendance, fees, and records. Cipher AI lives inside it: an assistant that reads intent, chooses a capability, checks structured data, and helps carry out a school operation.

Other products cover other jobs. Camsermar connects customers with service professionals. STUDCOM is a companion app for students. FinGer handles finance. Manka'a has to work for a restaurant, a customer, and a driver together. The longer accounts belong with the work. The shared habit is to look at a real workflow and build the system around it.

The useful version of AI, for me, is an assistant that can do a job inside a product.

Intelligence and interaction

AI, machine learning, and computer vision are now a large part of the practice. VisionDX is model work: preparing data, training, and exporting a malaria-detection model, with tools such as TensorFlow, Keras, EfficientNet, and ONNX. IsoID uses fingerprint and facial recognition for attendance. I also experiment with local models. The interest is intelligence that has a job inside a system.

Lola is more exploratory: a personal AI assistant for voice, local models, and computer vision. With a camera I have mapped hand and face movement onto a music player, changing tracks, volume, position in a song, and playlists by gesture. Air Piano turns finger movement into musical input. It is hands-on experimentation in how a person might operate software directly.

Teaching, and D-CAT

Founding JACKBASE TECH made the ownership plain. I decide what to build, I engineer it, and I stay with it when it fails. Teaching had already asked for the same kind of attention: a class that has to understand the idea, and a machine that has to work the next morning.

At D-CAT Center I am the Tech Engineer. I support the Website Design and Development track and the basic Mobile App Development track, both meant to prepare learners for real client work. The center helps people turn skills into market opportunities. My part is the technology, and the teaching beside it. I am still working where software engineering, AI, product development, and human interaction meet.
