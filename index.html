01 — About

A software engineer who ships the product.

I am a software engineer and product builder based in Cameroon. I founded JACKBASE TECH, and I lead the engineering there — taking ideas from a rough concept through interface, systems, and deployment.

The products span school platforms, marketplaces, finance tools, mobile apps, and AI. Some of that AI is an assistant working on structured data inside a real system. Some of it is computer vision trained for a specific task. Some of it is an experiment in how a person might control software with a hand, a face, or a voice.

Frontend is a large part of the craft: component structure, responsive interfaces, and interaction that feels considered. I also stay with the rest of the stack when the product needs it — APIs, data, authentication, and the path to production. I design, engineer, debug, and keep evolving software that people can actually use.
